Xenogerm Trader Stock

[h1]About[/h1]
In vanilla Biotech, the only ways to get a preset xenotype are to recruit one or to collect every gene and assemble the xenogerm yourself. Traders never sell the xenogerms that lore says must exist.

This mod adds complete xenogerms for preset xenotypes to trader inventories, so you can engage with the gene system early instead of spending the whole game hunting down genes.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]Xenogerm Trading[/h2]
[list]
[*]Exotic goods traders and imperial settlements stock xenogerms for preset xenotypes
[*]A purchased xenogerm gives the person that xenotype, so an ideoligion that prefers it will accept them
[*]Germline xenotypes such as impids are not sold by default. Enable them in the mod settings to make a person a born member whose children inherit the genes
[*]Expensive xenogerms are rarer by default. The rule can be changed in the mod settings
[*]The custom xenotypes of your starting colonists are sold too
[/list]

[h2]Pricing[/h2]
Prices can be changed in the mod settings. The defaults are:
[list]
[*]Pigskin, Impid, Dirtmole: 1500~1700 silver
[*]Hussar, Starjack, Highmate: 1700~2000 silver
[*]Sanguophage: ~3600 silver
[/list]
