Xenogerm Trader Stock

[h1]About[/h1]
In vanilla Biotech, the only ways to get a preset xenotype are to recruit one or to collect every gene and assemble the xenogerm yourself. Traders never sell the xenogerms that lore says must exist.

This mod adds complete xenogerms for preset xenotypes to trader inventories, so you can engage with the gene system early instead of spending the whole game hunting down genes.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]Xenogerm Trading[/h2]
[list]
[*]Exotic goods traders and imperial settlements stock xenogerms for preset xenotypes, modded xenotypes detected automatically
[*]Outlander settlements can be opted in from the stock quantity settings
[*]Purchased xenogerms assign xenotype identity on implantation, enabling ideology recognition
[*]Enable endogene-only xenotypes in mod settings to create naturalized germ-line members
[*]Configurable price-driven commonality: expensive xenogerms are rarer by default, or choose a different scaling
[*]Player-scenario xenotypes are treated as first-class citizens and included in trade-stock too
[/list]

[h2]Pricing[/h2]
Xenogerm prices configurable in settings, or take the defaults:
[list]
[*]Pigskin, Impid, Dirtmole: 1500~1700 silver
[*]Hussar, Starjack, Highmate: 1700~2000 silver
[*]Sanguophage: ~3600 silver
[/list]
